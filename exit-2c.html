About

The brand

Exit 2C was born from the underground dance floors of Miami, named after the real exit that takes you to Miami Ave, downtown. The spot where some of the city's rawest, most electric nights take shape.

First formed on Twitter as a method to break the ice within the existing house music scene, Exit 2C grew into a community. Friends of friends meeting and pursuing relationships outside of the entity which brought them together.

What started as a group of friends chasing self expression grew into a place where people feel welcome and build real connection — a home for the sound we love.

Today, Exit 2C is our events brand: the parties, the nights out, the dance floors where Miami's underground scene comes alive. Our music lives under Express Lane Records, the label side of what we do where we release tracks from the artists we believe in. Together, they're two halves of the same mission: give upcoming producers and DJs a real platform, and keep the sound moving forward.

We're not trying to be the best. We're building genuine community for the artists still on their way up, and for the people who show up every time just to move. If you've never been, welcome to Exit 2C. Your last exit before the party.
