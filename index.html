About

The imprint

Express Lane Records was created out of an abundance of passion to share, built because we wanted to contribute to the music we were championing on the dance floor, and our songs needed a home of their own.

What started as a way to formalize the sounds we loved, and continue to build upon, became a real platform for producers and DJs, founded on a belief in lifting others up the same way we have been. Not a vanity imprint, a launchpad.

Together with events brand Exit 2C, they're two halves of the same mission: shine a light on local talent and keep the sound moving forward.
