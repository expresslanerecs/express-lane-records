About

The imprint

Express Lane Records is an independent label imprint built around electro, acid, house, and other tech-forward sounds — a home for emerging producers whose work doesn't fit the press-safe mold. Alongside releases, the label runs an editorial interview series spotlighting Miami house artists, built on questions that get raw, unexpected answers.
